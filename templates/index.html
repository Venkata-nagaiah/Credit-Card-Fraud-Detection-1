🛡️ How Credit Card Fraud Detection Helps the Public & How to Stop Frauds

Practical protection mechanisms, automated card locks, and physical security safeguards protecting cardholders from financial fraud.

🔒

Automated Real-Time Card Freezes

Instant sub-second blocking on accounts undergoing abnormal velocity, high-value transfers, or total balance drain attacks.

💳

EMV Dynamic Cryptogram Tokenization

Every transaction generates a single-use crypto token, eliminating the risk of cloned magnetic stripe counterfeit cards.

🔔

Auditory & Push Buzzer Notifications

Direct audible warning feedback and immediate SMS / Mobile Bank authorization requests whenever balance discrepancies trigger flags.

📱

Two-Factor Multi-Tier Authentication

Enforces biometric and OTP confirmation for any transaction flagged with high balance deltas or exceeding threshold limits.
